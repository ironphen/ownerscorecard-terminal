## TIER 2 — Light passes, batched by fix type

**Batch 1 — one pipeline change, many shelves (do first).**
Derive grossProfit = Revenue − CostOfRevenue. Null rate 1.0 across the entire file; flagged by scouts on roughly fifteen shelves. Most damaging on Specialty Retail, Department Stores, Semiconductors, Pharma, Drug Distributors, Footwear, Food Products, Medical Devices. Several no-actions below are adequately served by this fix alone.

**Batch 2 — tag-mapping repairs (extraction is currently wrong).**
- Pipelines & Midstream: map PartnersCapital tags (equity null 0.42, strongest defect in Energy); surface equity-method JV income as its own line.
- E-Commerce: totalDebt null 0.31 repair; then 606 disaggregation to label which revenue ruler (1P gross vs take-rate) each filer uses.
- Construction Materials: totalDebt null 0.33 and 6.0 avg years; fix the record before any lens.
- Interactive Media & Platforms: single-filer capex tag repair (null 0.5 on 2 members).
- Biotechnology: render honest debt-free as zero, not null; lead presentation with runway and share-count dilution (already-pulled standard tags).
- Mortgage & Specialty Finance: demote GAAP income; book value per share as the headline series (standard tags).
- Brewers: fold in the queued STZ share-count fix.

**Batch 3 — single standard-tag additions (ASC 606 / 842 / ARO family) and desk inheritances.**
- Hotels & Resorts: strip cost-reimbursement revenue via 606 disaggregation (kills the Marriott low-margin illusion).
- Restaurants: franchised vs company-operated revenue split (606).
- Cruise Lines: customer deposits as visible float (contract-liability tags).
- Airlines: loyalty-program and air-traffic liability tags.
- Construction & Engineering: contract assets vs liabilities + RPO (inherits Desk 8 spec).
- Waste Management: ARO and accretion columns.
- Semiconductors: derived inventory-days and R&D-intensity columns (after Batch 1).
- Drug & Medical Distributors: derived supplier-float and margin-on-gross-profit view; arrangement, not new extraction.
- IT Services, Communications Equipment, Semiconductor Equipment: RPO/deferred-revenue columns inherited from Desk 4.
- Consumer Finance: credit-cost spec inherited from Desk 2 (FinancingReceivable tags).
- Gas + Water Utilities: Desk 7 tag set applied unchanged.
- Department & General Merchandise: membership-fee revenue line (606); comps inherited from Desk 10.
- Insurance L&H: tracked here, charter owned by Desk 1.

**Batch 4 — LIFO reserve (inventory footnote, standard-tagged).**
- Refining & Marketing: restate equity and inventory; billions of hidden asset backing at the big refiners.
- Food & Drug Retailing: LIFO reserve now; same-store-ex-fuel is text and waits for its shelf's turn in Notes.

**Batch 5 — text-sourced lenses; no deterministic extraction; do opportunistically when a shelf enters the Notes pipeline.**
Pharmaceuticals (top-product concentration), Health Care Providers (payor mix, same-facility volumes), Homebuilders (backlog, owned-vs-optioned lots), Telecom (service/equipment split, churn/ARPU), Media & Broadcasting (deal-amortization split for cash operating margin), Agricultural Products (RMI netted against short-term debt), Tobacco (volume decline vs price per unit), Brewers (aging inventory read as growth capex), Food Products (organic volume/price/mix), Capital Markets (AUM rollforward and net flows), Paper & Forest (timberland acres), Auto Dealers (gross profit by line), Real Estate Dev & Services (developer land at cost, segment fee split).

## Where scouts and defect signals disagree

1. **The clean-extraction trap.** Automobiles, Farm & Heavy Equipment, and Electric/Multi-Utilities show the healthiest profiles on the file (deep records, no anomalous nulls) yet scored 7-8. Null rates measure extraction health, not semantic truth. Desks 7 and 9 are funded against zero defect-signal support, on the scouts' economic argument alone.
2. **The inverse case.** Mortgage & Specialty Finance pairs the worst records in Financials (6.5 avg years, capex null 0.27) with a gap of 8; pool size ($7B, 11 members) demotes it to a Batch 2 repair. GapScore alone would have seated it.
3. **REITs is the one alignment.** Capex null 0.30 and the scout's economic argument point at exactly the same repair; the defect signal is evidence for the desk, not against it.
4. **Biotechnology.** totalDebt null 0.28 reinterpreted as honest zeros, not extraction failure; the fix is presentation, not tags.
5. **Casinos.** A standard-tag fix (842 leases) is within reach, yet the scout files no-action on pool and record thinness; allocation agrees.
6. **Insurance P&C capex null 0.26.** Confirmation of the template misfit the funded desk already addresses; not a new task.
7. **Gold & Precious Metals.** Scout marked full-desk while specifying it should ride the M&M desk; docketed as a rider, not a seat.
8. **Managed Care investmentIncome null 0.4** (best tagging in Health Care) corroborates the scout: these filers already speak insurance-tagged concepts the pipeline half-catches, which is why Desk 3 is cheap to stand up after Desk 1.

# The road ahead — as the partners see it

*2026-07-18. Decided by the partners — Ryan (managing), with the analysis — against the house
lens: Graham and Buffett's principles, with Munger in the room for the ownership stream (his
inclusion scoped and ratified). Companion to `docs/organized-owners.md` (the mission and its
research) and `docs/phase-2-plan.md` (the commercialization spec). This document is the sequence
and the reasoning; it is not a promise calendar. No section of it ships until it ships.*

## The vision, in one paragraph

OwnerScorecard becomes the place where the individual enterprising investor — Graham's word —
reads businesses the way an owner reads them, and stands with other owners the way the system
intended. The record makes every business legible; the publication (the Notes, the Ledger) earns
the living; the owners' area makes the ownership itself legible — the meetings, the votes, the
pay beside the record — and, in time, carries the community whose watching disciplines
managements before the fact. Mission, as ratified: *re-align managements with shareholders
through literacy and organization of the individual enterprising investor, re-establishing
themselves as owners of the business, as was intended.* The floor that justifies every step:
prevent the drowning-out from getting worse — or try.

## How the partners decide sequence

Four tests, applied to every candidate step, in order:

1. **Graham's margin of safety, applied to product.** Every step must be cheap to reverse and
   must prove demand before the next step commits. We probe with presentation surfaces before we
   build member surfaces; we build member surfaces before we build community; we never bet the
   franchise on an unproven layer. (This is how the pay table and the owners' area shipped —
   each a probe that stands alone.)
2. **Buffett's capital discipline.** Operations fund expansion. The Notes subscription is the
   revenue spine and it comes before the civic layer it will fund. The Motley Fool boards died
   because the community was never load-bearing for the business; ours will be load-bearing by
   construction — but the business comes first.
3. **The circle of competence.** We build what this shop is uniquely positioned to build: the
   record, the pipeline discipline, verdict-free presentation, community mechanics. We do not
   build proxy plumbing, we do not carry votes, we do not run campaigns, we do not do activism.
4. **Munger's inversion, per phase.** Before each phase: what would ruin it? The answer shapes
   the phase's gates (they are listed with each phase below).

And one clock: **ownership has seasons.** Proxy season runs roughly February–June; report season
runs January–April; the Berkshire meeting anchors early May. The 2026 season is behind us; the
2027 season is the fixed deadline for the season tools. Between now and then, the calendar —
not impatience — sets the order.

## The phases

### Phase I — the rails and the letters (now → autumn 2026)

*The revenue spine, and the publication doing its daily work. Nothing new in kind; everything
already ratified, now executed.*

- **The Notes cadence continues.** Capcom publishes on schedule; the alcohol-shelf note follows
  its fact-base and window; the Graham-1955 note awaits Ryan's hand. A new candidate joins the
  queue from this stream: the ownership essay — Graham's forty-year through-line
  (`docs/graham-owner-factbase.md` is citation-grade and waiting) — Ryan's call on whether and
  when; it would be the mission's founding prose in the publication's own voice.
- **Phase-2 rails: accounts and the paid tier.** Supabase + Stripe per the phase-2 spec,
  annual-first; the legal review of the paid-Notes stance precedes the first paid Note (standing
  requirement). This is the highest-leverage build in the plan because it serves both masters:
  it is the subscription rail, and it is the precondition for every member surface the ownership
  stream will ever want. *Inversion gate: churn is the killer — retention economics decide
  pricing and cadence; nothing ships that we can't sustain solo.*
- **The correctness campaign remains the standing discipline.** Recovery passes on the withheld
  filers (the ~110 structural withholds in the pay data; the say-on-pay format misses) continue
  opportunistically; every new surface inherits the full verification-and-gates treatment the
  last two days demonstrated. *A wrong number is worse than a missing one — the moat is this.*
- **The owners' area deepens quietly, off-season.** Same 8-Ks, same discipline, presentation
  only: director-election tallies and the other proposals beside the say-on-pay; proxy filings
  (DEF 14A) into the wire as they land; the forward meeting calendar (meeting dates are in the
  proxies) so /owners shows what is coming, not only what happened. Cheap, reversible, keeps the
  area alive between seasons. *Inversion gate: no judgment creep — a director's tally is a filed
  fact; the moment a surface needs an adjective, it doesn't ship.*

### Phase II — the season's tools (winter 2026–27)

*Built before the season needs them; nothing waits on an experiment.*

- **The Owner's Ballot.** For each covered company entering proxy season: the proxy's items
  presented verdict-free beside the record — the compensation item beside the pay-versus-record
  panel, the director slate beside the proxy's own disclosures, each shareholder proposal as the
  filing states it. Extraction follows the established discipline (parse, adversarially verify,
  gate, withhold ambiguity); presentation follows the established taste (the juxtaposition is
  the statement). Graham's closing plea in the last Intelligent Investor — that shareholders
  read proxy material from fellow-shareholders "with care" — is this surface's charter.
- **Notebook-public** (requires Phase I rails). The first member surface and the first social
  object: a reader may publish chosen notebook pages — their owner's memo per business, their
  dated notes. Solo value already exists (the private Notebook); this adds the public copy as an
  explicit act. *Inversion gate: the privacy tripwire holds absolutely — publishing is opt-in
  per page, holdings sizes never surface, and the private notebook never leaves the device.*
- **The area's name is settled by Ryan before the season** (the working "Owners" holds until
  his word replaces or confirms it).

### Phase III — the first season (spring 2027)

*The demand referendum. The season runs; we watch what owners actually do.*

- Proxy season live: the calendar fills, ballot pages publish as proxies land, the wire carries
  the season, the Notes can (at Ryan's discretion) read the season's notable items in the
  publication's voice.
- **The measures are honest and few**: do readers open the ballot pages; do notebook-public
  memos get written; does anyone arrive at the record THROUGH the owners' area. Not engagement
  metrics — evidence of owners behaving like owners. This season's evidence decides Phase IV's
  scope. *Inversion gate: a quiet season is information, not failure — the floor mission
  (prevent it getting worse) is served by the surfaces existing at all.*

### Phase IV — the community, seeded (from meeting season 2027, if the evidence warrants)

- **One room first** — where owner culture already lives (the Berkshire meeting is the obvious
  anchor and the calendar's gift) — before any general rollout. Rooms are anchored to the record,
  price-free by architecture, tenure-attested without sizes, moderated first by having nothing
  for speculators to eat. The status ladder is Ryan's design (his craft; compounding,
  behavior-based, never outcome-graded).
- **Declared votes** (the aggregate tally of members' stated intentions) follow the rooms, under
  the forum rules already mapped (14a-2(b)(6), operator immunity, accuracy discipline) — the
  community-of-records with civic teeth.
- *Inversion gates: the ASA capture trap (reader-only revenue is constitutional and permanent);
  archive permanence (rooms compile to static; the host can never delete the community's
  memory); the no-price rule is architecture before it is moderation.*

### The horizon (unscheduled, revisited each season)

- **The clerk** — each reader's AI reading filings and ballots against that reader's saved
  principles. Waits for rails, the ballot, and honest per-reader cost pricing.
- **International ownership layers** — UK/EU regimes differ (binding votes, different filings);
  nothing here before US depth is proven.
- **The neutral standing-instructions door** (the Exxon no-action path, reader-directed) —
  counsel-gated, partner-gated, years out, optional. Logged so it is not forgotten; not planned
  so it cannot distract.

## What we refuse — permanently, at roadmap level

No advertising. No issuer money, sponsorships, or "partnerships" with covered companies. No
price feeds, ratings, targets, or estimates. No vote-carrying, no campaigns, no house voting
recommendations. No engagement mechanics, no returns leaderboards. No coined feature names where
a plain noun serves. Reader-only revenue, always. Archives permanent, always.

## What we deliberately do not build next

Rooms before the season's evidence. The clerk before the rails. International governance before
US depth. A proxy-advice product in any form. Anything whose best justification is that a
competitor has it.

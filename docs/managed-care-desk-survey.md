# OWNERSCORECARD MANAGED-CARE DESK — MERGED TAG CENSUS + DRAFT SPEC SKELETON

*Third analyst-desk filing under the desks strategy: four Berkshire-schooled agents surveyed ten managed-care filers' XBRL disclosures (three size tiers plus a ratio-verification drill that recomputed MLR and DCP against filed 10-K text at UNH/HUM/CNC), then a senior synthesis merged the census. Desk protocol: survey -> THIS SPEC -> owner review -> extraction code -> hostile verify -> ship. Unlike the insurance and banks desks, Wave A ALREADY RUNS here (scripts/insuranceLines.mjs extracts every premiums-carrying filer), so this spec is part repair order, part extension: one live wrong-number-shaped defect (ELV development signs) and four known gaps are answered below. Pattern sources: docs/insurance-desk-survey.md, docs/banks-desk-survey.md.*

Evidence base: three tier surveys (10 filers) plus one derived-readings drill. Tier shorthand used throughout:
- MEGA: UNH, CI, ELV
- GOV (government-program large): CNC, HUM, MOH
- SMALL (young filers): OSCR, ALHC, CLOV, TRUP
- DRILL: the UNH/HUM/CNC ratio-verification survey (computed vs reported, to the tenth)

Confidence scale: HIGH (standard tag, long undimensioned series, gate available) / MEDIUM (seam-stitch or fallback chain required) / LOW (sparse, partial-scope, or per-filer map) / WITHHOLD (not extractable honestly from companyfacts).

School frame (binding): an MCO is an insurer in a health-services costume. MLR is the combined ratio's analog with a statutory floor; float is small and fast, so the tell is DAYS CLAIMS PAYABLE; prior-year development is the honesty meter; premiums and medical costs describe the insurance book — total revenue never does.

## 3. THE DERIVED READINGS (each with its withhold conditions)

1. MEDICAL LOSS RATIO vs THE STATUTORY FLOOR — claimsIncurred / premiumsEarned, framed against the ACA floors (80% individual/small group, 85% large group/Medicare): the spread between floor and filed ratio is the underwriting margin the law allows, and an MLR pinned near the floor is pricing discipline made visible. Verified to the tenth against filed text by the DRILL: UNH computed 83.17/85.55/89.15 vs reported MCR 83.2/85.5/89.1; HUM 87.28/89.80/90.22 vs 87.3/89.8/90.2 (2023 from the prior 10-K). Scope is honest by construction: the element pair excludes Optum/Evernorth (using Revenues would fabricate an 18-point-better UNH ratio; CI's Evernorth tags zero medical cost). LABEL: this is the GAAP ratio, not the ACA rebate MLR (which adds credibility adjustments and quality expenses) — the floor is a framing, never a compliance claim. WITHHOLD: CNC post-2017 (proxy fails by 1.6-2.8 points — the only large MCO whose MLR cannot be built pure); ALHC (structural); CLOV FY2021 unless segment-flagged (194% naive read — publish flagged, never averaged away: Graham's worst year counts); TRUP n/a.
2. DAYS CLAIMS PAYABLE — claims payable ÷ daily medical costs, the reserve-adequacy tell: a shrinking DCP while margins hold (or worsen) is tomorrow's earnings borrowed from the reserve. Convention: annual daily costs on the year's medical-cost total (lands within 1.5 days of CNC's own Q4-based print of 54/53/46 — basis labeled, Q7). FY23/24/25: UNH 48.9/47.3/45.7; HUM 42.3/37.9/32.8 (below the 40-50 norm and falling fast, with IBNR down in absolute dollars — the loudest Munger-inversion signal in the desk); CNC 55.3/53.2/47.5; ELV 45.1->42.1 into FY2025; MOH ~49; OSCR 67.5->53.0; CLOV 56.7->35.7 (net-basis label; at fast growers a falling DCP can be denominator growth — the meters disagree about tomorrow, so watch, don't conclude).

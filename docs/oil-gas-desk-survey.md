# OWNERSCORECARD OIL & GAS DESK — SURVEY (2026-07-26)

*Sixth analyst-desk filing. Seven Berkshire-schooled agents: a cohort audit, a live-defect hunt, three tag-census tiers, the ASC 932 reserves deep-dive, and a Munger traps lens. Desk protocol: survey -> THIS SPEC -> owner review -> extraction code -> hostile verify -> ship.*

*STATUS: the COHORT half is SHIPPED (commit e70d010). The extraction half is FILED, NOT RATIFIED — no lines are built.*

**The frame:** a producer sells its own inventory and must buy it back. Every barrel produced is a barrel gone, so the only question that matters over a cycle is whether the company replaces what it sells for less than it sells it for. Depletion is not an accounting abstraction here, it is the cost of goods sold, which is exactly why EBITDA is the most dangerous number in this industry.

---

## 1. THE COHORT — SHIPPED

Forty companies moved across twelve shelves, and two shelves were created that the taxonomy never had.

**Integrated Oil & Gas (new, 12 members).** Exxon, Chevron, BP, Equinor and Suncor carry SIC 2911 (petroleum refining); Shell, Total and Eni carry 1311 (crude petroleum). Same business model, split by a filing code, so the five largest energy companies on the site sat on different shelves and could not be compared with one another.

**Oil & Gas Royalties & Mineral Interests (new, 7 members).** A mineral-interest owner collects a share of production and bears no development capital. Texas Pacific Land was verified to carry neither successful-efforts nor full-cost property tags and no development-cost tag of any kind. Ranked against operators on return on capital, a landlord beats a factory by construction.

The rest were ordinary consequences of stale codes: Hess is a pure-play producer carrying a refining code; Baytex and HighPeak are producers filed under drilling; TETRA is a services firm with no production; Hess Midstream owns no reserves; Sasol's chemicals segments outweigh its fuels.


## 2. RESERVES — THE DESK'S CENTREPIECE

### Verdict

THE DESK CAN EXIST, BUT NOT AS A COHORT COLUMN — AND THE REIT PRECEDENT APPLIES ALMOST EXACTLY, WITH ONE IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE.

The difference first, because it decides the shape of the answer. FFO was not in XBRL at all, for anyone, and the honest response was to withhold it universally. Proved reserves are not like that. They ARE tagged, in a standard taxonomy, with an exact and stable vocabulary — but only by some filers, and the ones who decline are the ones who matter most. So the finding is not "withhold from everyone." It is "publish where filed, withhold where not, and never impute" — which is harder to build and much harder to resist fudging, because the empty cells will sit next to full ones on the same shelf.

THE CENSUS. I examined 51 producers (the repo's 47 SIC-1311 rows plus XOM, CVX, COP, and re-checks of APA, VTLE, AMPY, BRY, MRO, PXD, SWN, CPE, ESTE), reading companyfacts for each and downloading the FY2025 primary 10-K document for 46 of them to distinguish "untagged" from "hidden behind a dimension."
- 21 have the complete set: total proved, PD/PUD split, production, and a rollforward. EQT, EXE, AR, CNX, OVV, CIVI, CHRD, GPOR, BKV, VNOM, BSM, SD, TXO, REPX, HPK, INR, APA, VTLE, DVN, FANG, NUAI.
- 11 have fragments — a standardized measure with no quantities, or a PD balance with no rollforward: RRC, MUR, NOG, TALO, CRK, MNR, AMPY, GTE, WTI, KRP, CRGY.
- 19 have nothing at all, and they include every one of the five largest: XOM, CVX, COP, OXY, EOG, plus CTRA, MTDR, PR, SM, CRC, MGY, EGY, DMLP, BRY, SOC, GRNT, DEC, VTS, KOS.
By the repo's own FY2025 revenue the readable 21 are $85bn against $640bn unreadable — 11% coverage. Strip the majors' refining and it is still a minority: COP $52bn, EOG $23bn, OXY $22bn, CTRA $7bn, PR $5bn, MTDR $4bn, SM $3bn, CRC $3bn and MGY $1bn of pure exploration-and-production revenue sit in the dark.

(1) ARE QUANTITIES TAGGED? Yes, where tagged, in the srt namespace, and I confirmed every element below by reading it out of filings I fetched this session. Instants: srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReserveNetEnergy, srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesNet, srt:ProvedDevelopedReservesBOE1, srt:ProvedDevelopedReservesVolume, srt:ProvedUndevelopedReserveBOE1, srt:ProvedUndevelopedReserveVolume. Durations: srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReserveProductionEnergy, ...ExtensionAndDiscoveryEnergy, ...RevisionOfPreviousEstimateEnergy, ...PurchaseOfMineralInPlaceEnergy, srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesSaleOfMineralInPlaceEnergy, and the non-Energy siblings srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesProduction / ...ExtensionsDiscoveriesAndAdditions / ...RevisionsOfPreviousEstimatesIncreaseDecrease / ...PurchasesOfMineralsInPlace / ...SalesOfMineralsInPlace. Older filings use identical local names under us-gaap. THE DIMENSIONAL POINT THE BRIEF ANTICIPATED IS HALF RIGHT: per-product legs ARE dimension-locked, on srt:ReserveQuantitiesByTypeOfReserveAxis with members srt:CrudeOilMember / srt:OilReservesMember / srt:NaturalGasReservesMember / srt:NaturalGasLiquidsReservesMember, and companyfacts drops them — the Tier-2 keyhole is required for oil-versus-gas detail. But the BOE and Mcfe TOTALS sit in the default context on the *Energy and *BOE1 elements, so the numbers the reader actually needs are Tier-1 reachable. Geography axes I did not find in use at any FY2025 filer I read. Units are the real hazard: I counted MMcfe, Bcfe, Mcfe, MMcf, MMBbls, MBbls, bbl, MBoe, MMBoe, Boe and, at one filer, mJ. FANG declares MBoe and applies scale='3', so companyfacts serves 3,617,856,000 "MBoe" for 3.6 billion barrels — a thousandfold overstatement served by the SEC's own API. EQT files a duplicate production fact in Bcfe inflated the same way. The unit tag cannot be trusted; the identities can.

(2) IS THE STANDARDIZED MEASURE TAGGED? Yes, and better than the quantities — 36 of 42 filers I tested carry it, and where present the disclosure is near-complete: srt:StandardizedMeasureOfDiscountedFutureNetCashFlowsRelatingToProvedOilAndGasReserves plus all six build-up components (srt:FutureNetCashFlowsRelatingToProvedOilAndGasReserves{CashInflows, ProductionCosts, DevelopmentCosts, IncomeTaxExpense, NetCashFlows, TenPercentAnnualDiscountForEstimatedTimingOfCashFlows}) and the six-line rollforward (srt:SalesAndTransfersOfOilAndGasProducedNetOfProductionCosts, srt:NetIncreaseDecreaseInSalesAndTransferPricesAndProductionCosts, srt:ExtensionsDiscoveriesAdditionsAndImprovedRecoveryLessRelatedCosts, srt:StandardizedMeasureOfDiscountedFutureNetCashFlowRelatingToProvedOilAndGasReservesAccretionOfDiscount, srt:IncreaseDecreaseInFutureIncomeTaxExpenseEstimatesOnFutureCashFlowsRelatedToProvedOilAndGasReserves, srt:StandardizedMeasureOfDiscountedFutureNetCashFlowOfProvedOilAndGasReservesOther). 24 of 36 carry all six of each. The same 19 that tag no quantities tag no standardized measure. AND WE SHOULD BE SLOW TO USE IT. EQT's went $40.1bn (2022) to $9.3bn (2023) to $8.0bn (2024) to $21.3bn (2025) on a reserve base that barely moved: the number is measuring the SEC's mandated twelve-month trailing price average, not the business. Exxon says so itself in the filing I read, in as many words. If it appears at all it should appear as the rollforward, where the price line and the volume lines are separated and the reader can see which did the work — never as a headline, never as PV-10, never divided by shares.

(3) THE DECISIVE QUESTION. RESERVE LIFE AND RESERVE REPLACEMENT ARE HONESTLY COMPUTABLE — for the filers who tag, gated by two identities, and for nobody else. This is materially better than FFO, because unlike FFO these are not definitions we would be inventing; they are two filed facts divided, with the filer's own rollforward available to prove the facts belong together.

THE ARITHMETIC, EQT FY2025, every input a filed fact, verified against the printed table in eqt-20251231.htm:
  Reserve life = 28,046,479 MMcfe / 2,382,367 MMcfe = 11.77 years.
  Component identity: PD 20,580,992 + PUD 7,465,487 = 28,046,479. Exact.
  Rollforward identity: 26,264,669 + 2,444,717 - 27,073 + 1,768,560 - 22,027 - 2,382,367 = 28,046,479. Exact, to the last digit filed.
  Product identity at 6 Mcf per Boe: 26,416,290 + 259,978x6 + 11,720x6 = 28,046,478 against 28,046,479. One unit in twenty-eight million.
  Reserve replacement, all sources = (2,444,717 - 27,073 + 1,768,560 - 22,027) / 2,382,367 = 174.8%.
  Reserve replacement, drill bit only = (2,444,717 - 27,073) / 2,382,367 = 101.5%.
Those last two are the finding in miniature. The headline 175% is mostly the Olympus Energy acquisition; strip bought barrels and EQT replaced its own production by one and a half percent. Both belong on the page and the second is the one that answers the frame's question.
And FANG FY2025 as the second case, identities also exact: life 10.76 years, replacement 118.0% all-in but 81.8% from the drill bit, because revisions of -304,085 MBoe erased more than half of the 578,919 MBoe added by extension. Diamondback did not replace what it sold.
THE GATES ARE NOT DECORATION. Run with a generic tag-fallback picker across the 21 readable filers, the rollforward closed for 11 and failed for 8 — CNX by 170,747,000 Mcfe (1.8%), GPOR, REPX, CIVI, BKV, HPK, DVN. Some of those are my picker's mapping gaps rather than filer error; the point is that we cannot tell which without a per-filer map, and a number we cannot tell about does not ship. Publish only where both identities close, and mark the rest missing.

(4) FINDING AND DEVELOPMENT COST PER BARREL. COMPUTABLE FOR ROUGHLY 20 FILERS, AND IT IS THE BEST NUMBER ON THE PAGE — with a definitional discipline that must be settled before a line of code is written. The inputs are tagged: us-gaap:CostsIncurredDevelopmentCosts, us-gaap:CostsIncurredExplorationCosts, us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfOilAndGasPropertiesWithProvedReserves, us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfUnprovedOilAndGasProperties, and where filers bother, us-gaap:CostsIncurredOilAndGasPropertyAcquisitionExplorationAndDevelopmentActivities. 20 of the 21 quantity-complete filers have enough of them.
  EQT: drillbit F&D = (1,725,438 + 3,601) / (2,444,717 - 27,073) = $0.715 per Mcfe, or $4.29 per Boe. All-sources = 3,642,011 / 4,186,204 = $0.870 per Mcfe, $5.22 per Boe. Set against filed cash margin of $2.427 per Mcfe (SalesAndTransfersOfOilAndGasProducedNetOfProductionCosts / production) and filed depletion of $0.950 per Mcfe. EQT bought its inventory back for seventy-two cents and sold it for $2.43 before depletion, and its accounting depletion charge is higher than its cash replacement cost. That single line is the whole thesis of the desk, and it is entirely filed.
  FANG: drillbit F&D = (3,613 + 212) / 274,834 = $13.92 per Boe, corroborated within six percent by filed depletion of $14.70. Ignore revisions and the same filer's F&D "improves" to $6.61 — half the truth. So the definition is not a detail; excluding revisions is the standard way this number is made to lie, and the desk must include them and say so.
  THREE HARD CONSTRAINTS. First, unproved-property acquisitions buy no proved reserves, so an all-sources ratio that puts FANG's $5,226m of unproved acquisition in the numerator produces $29.47 per Boe and means nothing. Publish drillbit F&D as the headline and all-sources beside it, never one alone. Second, F&D is the one output the unit defect can corrupt, because it is dollars over volume and the scale does not cancel; it needs a magnitude gate — filed revenue per unit and filed depletion per unit must both land in a sane band or the cell is withheld. Third, the element names cannot be trusted: FANG puts its $13,659m total on us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfOilAndGasProperties, an acquisition-only element, and puts $102m on the correct total element. Only the component-sum identity catches that.

(5) PUD SHARE. READABLE, EXACT, AND THE CHEAPEST HONEST NUMBER WE CAN PUBLISH. srt:ProvedUndevelopedReserveBOE1 or srt:ProvedUndevelopedReserveVolume over the total, with srt:ProvedDevelopedReservesBOE1 as the closing check — and that check passed for 20 of 21 filers, failing only GPOR by 0.02%, which is rounding in the filer's own table. EQT 26.6%, FANG 30.3%, APA 30.5%, OVV 35.8%, GPOR 43.5%, REPX 41.2%, HPK 44.7%, INR 55.4% against BSM at 12.2%. That spread is the promise-versus-fact distinction made numerical, it requires no definition of ours, and it is the one reserve figure that needs no gate beyond its own addition. Some filers go further — FANG tags a fifteen-line PUD-only rollforward on fang: extensions including conversions to developed and price-driven revisions — but that is extension-namespace territory and belongs behind the Wells Fargo precedent, not in a first release.

TWO FINDINGS I DID NOT EXPECT, BOTH LOAD-BEARING.
THE ACCOUNTING METHOD IS MACHINE-READABLE, AND THE SUPPLEMENTAL DISCLOSURE IS WHERE THE TWO METHODS MEET. I tested 37 producers. The element pair us-gaap:OilAndGasPropertySuccessfulEffortMethod* versus us-gaap:OilAndGasPropertyFullCostMethod* / us-gaap:CapitalizedCostsOfUnprovedPropertiesExcludedFromAmortizationCumulative decides 26 of them, and for the other 11 the prose is unanimous and one-sided in every single case (XOM, CVX, COP, OXY, CTRA, CRC, MUR successful efforts; VNOM, MTDR, TALO, NOG full cost). So the hazard the brief called central is not merely a caveat we print — it is a fact we can carry, and the terminal can refuse to rank a full-cost filer against a successful-efforts one on any earnings or return measure. And here is the useful half: ASC 932's supplemental disclosures are method-NEUTRAL. Reserve quantities, costs incurred and the standardized measure are computed the same way under both. So reserve life, replacement, PUD share and F&D per barrel are the only figures in this industry that CAN be compared across the divide — while net income, capitalized cost, depletion per barrel and every return built on them cannot. That inverts the usual instinct. The supplemental schedule, the part most readers skip, is the only comparable page in the filing.
THE MTDR CASE IS THE DESK'S MATADOR MOMENT. Matador tags srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesNet exactly once in its FY2025 10-K, on the 260 MMcf-per-day inlet capacity of the Marlan Processing Plant. A blind sweep for that element name publishes a gas plant's throughput as a company's proved reserves. That is the oil-and-gas analogue of handing FFO to Howard Hughes, and it settles the architecture: named targets with mapped coordinates and closing identities, never a taxonomy sweep.

WHAT I WOULD PUT TO THE OWNER. Ship the reserve block for the filers who file it, with both identities as hard gates and every cell absent where they do not close. Do NOT compute, estimate, interpolate or carry forward a reserve figure for XOM, CVX, COP, OXY, EOG, CTRA, MTDR, PR, SM, CRC or MGY — publish instead what those companies DO file in structured form, which for the majors is capitalized proved and unproved property cost, suspended exploratory well costs, and depletion, and say plainly on the page that the reserve disclosure exists in the filing and is not tagged. That is the REIT desk's answer transplanted: withhold the industry's headline where it is not filed, publish the filed cash and cost instead, and let the absence be visible rather than papered over. The one thing that must not happen is what the FFO code did — a `|| 0` or a fallback that turns a missing barrel into a zero barrel, or a stale 2021 EOG reserve base divided by 2025 production. EOG in particular needs a staleness gate keyed to the reserve fact's OWN period end, because its facts did not vanish, they froze, and frozen facts are the ones that get published by accident.


### Proposed lines

All names below are proposals for a lines vocabulary; every source element is one I confirmed by reading data fetched this session, exact spelling. NOTHING here should be computed unless the two identity gates close for that filer-year, and every line is nullable-by-default with no fallback to zero.

TIER 1 — undimensioned, reachable from companyfacts, ~21 producers:
  provedReservesTotal — srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReserveNetEnergy, else srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesNet. Store the filer's declared unit AND a normalized boe6 value; never display the raw XBRL magnitude without the gate below.
  provedDevelopedReserves — srt:ProvedDevelopedReservesBOE1, else srt:ProvedDevelopedReservesVolume, else srt:ProvedDevelopedReservesBOE.
  provedUndevelopedReserves — srt:ProvedUndevelopedReserveBOE1, else srt:ProvedUndevelopedReserveVolume, else srt:ProvedUndevelopedReserveBOE.
  reservesProduction — srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReserveProductionEnergy, else srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesProduction. The denominator of everything.
  reservesExtensionsDiscoveries — srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReserveExtensionAndDiscoveryEnergy, else ...ReservesExtensionsDiscoveriesAndAdditions.
  reservesRevisions — srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReserveRevisionOfPreviousEstimateEnergy, else ...ReservesRevisionsOfPreviousEstimatesIncreaseDecrease. Signed; never dropped.
  reservesPurchasesInPlace — srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservePurchaseOfMineralInPlaceEnergy, else ...ReservesPurchasesOfMineralsInPlace.
  reservesSalesInPlace — srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesSaleOfMineralInPlaceEnergy, else ...ReservesSalesOfMineralsInPlace.
  standardizedMeasure — srt:StandardizedMeasureOfDiscountedFutureNetCashFlowsRelatingToProvedOilAndGasReserves. Present only inside its rollforward, never as a headline, never per share, never called PV-10 or value.
  smogFutureCashInflows / smogFutureProductionCosts / smogFutureDevelopmentCosts / smogFutureIncomeTax / smogFutureNetCashFlows / smogTenPercentDiscount — srt:FutureNetCashFlowsRelatingToProvedOilAndGasReserves{CashInflows, ProductionCosts, DevelopmentCosts, IncomeTaxExpense, NetCashFlows, TenPercentAnnualDiscountForEstimatedTimingOfCashFlows}.
  smogPriceChange — srt:NetIncreaseDecreaseInSalesAndTransferPricesAndProductionCosts. The line that shows how much of a standardized-measure move was price rather than barrels; arguably the single most honest item in the whole schedule.
  smogSalesNetOfProductionCosts — srt:SalesAndTransfersOfOilAndGasProducedNetOfProductionCosts.
  smogAccretion — srt:StandardizedMeasureOfDiscountedFutureNetCashFlowRelatingToProvedOilAndGasReservesAccretionOfDiscount.
  smogExtensionsLessCosts — srt:ExtensionsDiscoveriesAdditionsAndImprovedRecoveryLessRelatedCosts.
  smogTaxChange — srt:IncreaseDecreaseInFutureIncomeTaxExpenseEstimatesOnFutureCashFlowsRelatedToProvedOilAndGasReserves.
  smogOther — srt:StandardizedMeasureOfDiscountedFutureNetCashFlowOfProvedOilAndGasReservesOther.
  costsIncurredDevelopment — us-gaap:CostsIncurredDevelopmentCosts.
  costsIncurredExploration — us-gaap:CostsIncurredExplorationCosts.
  costsIncurredAcqProved — us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfOilAndGasPropertiesWithProvedReserves.
  costsIncurredAcqUnproved — us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfUnprovedOilAndGasProperties.
  costsIncurredTotal — the SUM of the four above. Do NOT read us-gaap:CostsIncurredOilAndGasPropertyAcquisitionExplorationAndDevelopmentActivities or us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfOilAndGasProperties as the total: FANG puts $102m on the first and its $13,659m total on the second. If a filer tags a total element, use it only as a CHECK against the sum, and withhold the year if they disagree.
  producingRevenue / producingLiftingCosts / producingDepletion / producingNetIncome / producingImpairment — us-gaap:ResultsOfOperationsRevenueFromOilAndGasProducingActivities, us-gaap:ResultsOfOperationsProductionOrLiftingCosts, us-gaap:ResultsOfOperationsDepreciationDepletionAndAmortizationAndValuationProvisions, us-gaap:ResultsOfOperationsOilAndGasProducingActivitiesNetIncomeExcludingCorporateOverheadAndInterestCosts, us-gaap:ResultsOfOperationsImpairmentOfOilAndGasProperties. Available for about 15 filers; carries the segment-level economics without corporate overhead.
  capitalizedCostsProved / capitalizedCostsUnproved / capitalizedCostsAccumDDA — us-gaap:CapitalizedCostsProvedProperties, us-gaap:CapitalizedCostsUnprovedProperties, us-gaap:CapitalizedCostsAccumulatedDepreciationDepletionAmortizationAndValuationAllowanceForRelatingToOilAndGasProducingActivities. THESE ARE AVAILABLE FOR SEVERAL MAJORS (COP tags both proved and unproved) and are the honest fallback content for the untagged nineteen.
  suspendedExploratoryWellCosts — us-gaap:CapitalizedExploratoryWellCostAdditionsPendingDeterminationOfProvedReserves. Tagged by XOM, CVX, COP, EOG. Successful-efforts only, so it doubles as a method signal.
  accountingMethod — enum successfulEfforts | fullCost | undetermined. Resolve from us-gaap:OilAndGasPropertySuccessfulEffortMethodGross / ...Net / us-gaap:ProvedOilAndGasPropertySuccessfulEffortMethod versus us-gaap:OilAndGasPropertyFullCostMethodGross / ...Net / ...Depletion / us-gaap:CapitalizedCostsOfUnprovedPropertiesExcludedFromAmortizationCumulative; fall back to unanimous prose only, and return undetermined on any conflict. This gates comparability, not just a footnote.

TIER 2 — keyhole targets, dimension-locked, invisible to companyfacts:
  provedReservesOil / provedReservesGas / provedReservesNgl — same srt element names above, contexts carrying srt:ReserveQuantitiesByTypeOfReserveAxis with srt:CrudeOilMember or srt:OilReservesMember, srt:NaturalGasReservesMember, srt:NaturalGasLiquidsReservesMember. Exact-member-set required; a context bearing any other axis yields nothing. Worth doing only because the product mix decides whether a "boe" is oil or six times its heating value in gas.

DERIVED, published only behind the gates:
  reserveLife = provedReservesTotal / reservesProduction. Years.
  reserveReplacementDrillbit = (extensions + revisions) / production. The headline; revisions ALWAYS included.
  reserveReplacementAllSources = (extensions + revisions + purchases - sales) / production. Beside it, never instead of it.
  pudShare = provedUndevelopedReserves / provedReservesTotal.
  fdCostDrillbit = (costsIncurredDevelopment + costsIncurredExploration) / (extensions + revisions), per boe.
  fdCostAllSources = costsIncurredTotal / (extensions + revisions + purchases), per boe.
  replacementMargin = producingRevenue-per-unit less liftingCosts-per-unit less fdCostDrillbit. The frame's question as one number: what the company clears after buying back what it sold.

GATES, all mandatory, all withhold-on-failure:
  G1 componentIdentity — provedDeveloped + provedUndeveloped = provedReservesTotal within 0.01%. Passed 20 of 21; catches unit and period mismatches.
  G2 rollforwardClosure — priorTotal + extensions + revisions + purchases - sales - production = provedReservesTotal within 0.01%. Passed 11 of 19 tested blind; CNX misses by 170,747,000 Mcfe on an untagged line. This gate alone is the difference between the desk and the FFO defect.
  G3 unitMagnitude — producingRevenue / production must fall in a plausible per-boe band and producingDepletion / production likewise, else all per-unit dollar lines are withheld. Required because FANG's companyfacts values are a thousandfold overstated by a scale attribute that contradicts its own unit label.
  G4 sameUnit — every input to a ratio must come from the identical declared unit string. EQT files the same production quantity in both MMcfe (correct) and Bcfe (inflated 1000x); mixing them silently produces a thousandfold error.
  G5 freshness — the reserve fact's OWN period end must match the fiscal year being displayed. EOG's reserve facts stop at 2021-12-31 while it keeps filing 10-Ks; without this the terminal serves a four-year-old reserve base against current production and nothing looks wrong.
  G6 noNarrativeFacts — reject any candidate whose context or magnitude marks it as a sentence fragment rather than a schedule line. EQT's srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReserveExtensionAndDiscoveryEnergy [Bcfe] = 133,000 is a clause from a PUD paragraph; MTDR's only srt:ProvedDevelopedAndUndevelopedReservesNet is a gas plant's daily inlet capacity. A magnitude sanity band against production catches both.
  G7 methodBarrier — never rank or median a full-cost filer against a successful-efforts filer on any earnings, capitalized-cost or return measure. Reserve quantities, costs incurred, the standardized measure and everything derived from them are method-neutral and MAY be compared; net income, depletion per barrel and capitalized cost may not.


### Per filer

### EBITDA in an extractive business — depletion IS cost of goods sold
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED as an industry hazard, REFUTED as a current live defect on our surfaces — with one dormant landmine and one live equivalent that is worse
- **Example:** Diamondback FY2025 prices the fallacy: production 336,178 MBOE, oil/gas/NGL sales $13,453M, production costs $3,231M, depletion of oil and gas properties $4,943M at an average depletion rate of $14.60 per BOE, plus a $3,652M ceiling-test impairment — leaving results of operations from producing activities of $1,265M after tax (10-K Note 18). ### The ceiling test — a formula at a mandated price is not an impairment
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED, and the cleanest single exhibit in the survey: two Permian producers, same year, same prices, $3.7bn apart
- **Example:** Diamondback FY2025 (full cost, 10-K Note 5): 'As a result of the decline in commodity prices during 2025, the Company recorded a non-cash ceiling test impairment for the year ended December 31, 2025 of $3.7 billion... The impairment charge affected the Company's reported net income but did not reduce its cash flow.' The ceiling is defined in the filing as future net revenues discounted at 10% 'based on the trailing 12-month unweighted average of the first-day-of-the-month price' — a mandated convention, not a judgment. Devon FY2025 (successful efforts, same Permian rock, same calendar year, same strip): oil and gas impairment of ZERO. Devon's only FY2025 asset impairment was $254M and its own MD&A says it arose because 'Devon rationalized two headquarters-related real estate assets' — office buildings, not wells. So on our surfaces, in FY2025, FANG shows a $3,652M charge and DVN shows nothing, and the difference is the accounting method, not the geology. The balance-sheet consequence compounds: FANG's Note 18 shows total oil and gas properties $95,529M against accumulated depletion $(15,974)M AND accumulated impairment $(11,606)M — 12% of gross cost has been ceiling-tested away, permanently shrinking the depletion base and the invested-capital denominator, so a full-cost filer's ROIC and margins IMPROVE after a cyclical writedown. The charge is also reversible in economics but not in accounting: FANG's own risk language warns that a further decline in the trailing 12-month average 'may' force more write-downs, and the same trailing average recovering does not write the asset back up.
- **XBRL:** us-gaap:ImpairmentOfOilAndGasProperties is present for 13 of 15 sampled and current for FANG ($3,652M FY2025), EOG ($843M), TALO ($454M), NOG ($703M), MUR ($115M), CRK ($29M), AR ($29M), RRC ($0M) — but it is a single concept used for two economically different events, with NOTHING in the fact to say which. us-gaap:ResultsOfOperationsImpairmentOfOilAndGasProperties (6 of 15) is no better. The ceiling-test price basis itself is text-only or a company extension: FANG tags it as fang:EstimatedFutureNetRevenueDiscountedRatePerAnnumBasedOn12MonthsTrailingUnweightedAverageFirstOfMonthPrice in the inline instance — a custom concept no other filer shares. There is no standard tag that marks a charge as ceiling-test-driven.
- **Gate:** Do not publish a bare 'impairments' figure on this shelf. Label it by the method already resolved in trap 1: on a full-cost filer render the line as 'Ceiling-test writedown (full cost, at the mandated trailing 12-month average price)'; on a successful-efforts filer render it as 'Asset impairment (successful efforts)'. Never median, rank or sum the two together. Add a second, separate line on full-cost filers — cumulative ceiling writedowns, from the CapitalizedCosts... accumulated tags — because it is the amount by which the return denominator has been reduced by convention rather than by operations, and an owner reading ROIC deserves to see it. Where the method is undetermined, withhold the impairment line rather than imply the charge means one thing.

### Owner-earnings frame for a producer

THE FRAME, STATED AS ARITHMETIC. A producer's owner earnings are the field cash margin, less the capital actually required to put back the barrels it sold, less the corporate costs and the abandonment accrual that the field statement excludes. Depletion is not an adjustment to that figure; it is a backward-looking proxy FOR the replacement term, computed on what the barrels cost when they were booked rather than what the next ones cost, and it must be REPLACED by the replacement term, not added back to it. Buffett's formula for a manufacturer deducts the capex needed to hold unit volume and competitive position; for a producer that capex has a name on the filing — costs incurred in oil and gas property acquisition, exploration and development — and it is disclosed under ASC 932 by every filer, under either accounting method.

TERM 1 — FIELD CASH MARGIN (production revenue less production costs). HONEST AND EXTRACTABLE, with a coverage gate. Best source, method-neutral, filed inside the standardized-measure roll-forward: srt:SalesAndTransfersOfOilAndGasProducedNetOfProductionCosts, present for 11 of 15 sampled and current for 10 (FANG $10,222M FY2025, DVN $7,656M, TALO $1,233M, AR $1,855M, MUR $1,686M, NOG $1,476M, CRK $1,099M, CIVI $3,807M FY2024, WTI $168M, SD $110M). Verified against the filings: FANG's $13,453M of oil/gas/NGL sales less $3,231M of production costs is exactly the tagged $10,222M; Devon's is exactly the $7,656M its MD&A calls 'field-level cash margin' at $24.97 per Boe. Second source where the first is missing: us-gaap:ResultsOfOperationsRevenueFromOilAndGasProducingActivities (5 of 15 current). CAUTION: the sign convention on the roll-forward line is inconsistent (EOG tags it negative), so take absolute value and tripwire a within-company sign flip. WITHHOLD where neither concept is present — do not substitute us-gaap:Revenues, which for Devon includes $5,563M of marketing resale and $402M of derivatives on top of $11,223M of actual production.

TERM 2 — THE REPLACEMENT CHARGE. HONEST AND EXTRACTABLE IN DOLLARS, NEVER IN BARRELS. Use costs incurred, split and summed from the four components confirmed this session: us-gaap:CostsIncurredDevelopmentCosts + us-gaap:CostsIncurredExplorationCosts (the drill bit) and us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfOilAndGasPropertiesWithProvedReserves + us-gaap:CostsIncurredAcquisitionOfUnprovedOilAndGasProperties (the checkbook). 11-12 of 15 coverage, current to FY2025 for most. FY2025: FANG $3,825M drill bit and $9,834M purchased, total $13,659M; DVN $3,638M drill bit and $362M purchased, total $4,000M. NEVER take the total from us-gaap:CostsIncurredOilAndGasPropertyAcquisitionExplorationAndDevelopmentActivities — FANG tags that concept at $102M against a filed total of $13,659M — sum the components and use the total concept only as a tripwire. Method caveat, measured not assumed: Devon's FY2016 costs incurred were $3,132M as filed under full cost and $2,884M as recast under successful efforts, an 8% residue from capitalized overhead. That is an order of magnitude smaller than the 47% EPS divergence the method causes elsewhere, which is precisely why this is the term to build on.

TERM 3 — CORPORATE COSTS BELOW THE FIELD LINE. The producing-activities schedule explicitly excludes them; FANG's note says so ('It does not include any interest costs or general and administrative costs'), as does Devon's. Deduct filed G&A and net financing costs from the company income statement, and note that G&A is itself method-sensitive (Devon FY2017: $682M under full cost, $872M under successful efforts, because full cost capitalizes the difference into the pool). Where the method is undetermined, say so beside the figure.

TERM 4 — THE ABANDONMENT ACCRUAL. Deduct accretion, do not ignore the stock. us-gaap:AssetRetirementObligationAccretionExpense is present and current for 15 of 15 sampled (FANG $35M, DVN $51M, TALO $125M, MUR $58M, OXY $212M FY2025). Show the balance beside equity from us-gaap:AssetRetirementObligation; flag where it exceeds equity (W&T Offshore: ARO $562M against equity of −$199.8M).

WHAT CANNOT BE HONESTLY COMPUTED, AND MUST STAY WITHHELD.
(1) ANY PER-BARREL FIGURE. Finding and development cost per BOE, reserve replacement ratio, reserve life, depletion per BOE, PUD share, SMOG per BOE. The dollar numerators are clean; the barrel denominators are not. The reserve-quantity units in the srt namespace are wrong or non-comparable in nearly every filer I checked — FANG tags 3,617,856,000 with unit 'MBoe' for a filed 3,617,856 MBOE (1,000x), DVN tags 2,428 with unit 'MMcfe' for a filed 2,428 MMBoe (a 6x energy-unit error), MUR tags 418,900,000 with unit 'MMBbls' (1e6), WTI tags total proved in 'MMBTU' for two years then 'MMBoe', AR and FANG each change their own unit label mid-series with no change in value scale. There is no defensible normalisation: the unit string does not describe the value and there is no second field that does. This is the desk's REIT-shaped answer — the industry's signature measure exists in the filing and must be withheld from structured extraction. The dollars go out; the barrels do not.
(2) MAINTENANCE CAPEX AS A SEPARATE NUMBER. The filings do not disclose the capital required to hold production flat. FANG discloses $3.6bn spent developing PUDs in 2025 and $7.7bn of future PUD development, Devon discloses $1.1bn to convert 175 MMBoe — but neither says what it would cost to stand still. Publish total costs incurred with the drill-bit/purchased split and let the reader judge; do not invent a maintenance figure, and specifically do not keep using depreciation as the stand-in on this shelf (src/lib/fundamentals.mjs:572-587), because for a producer that stand-in IS the depletion rate on the old cost basis.
(3) A NORMALISED OR MID-CYCLE OWNER EARNINGS. Every input moves with a price we are barred from forecasting. Publish the filed year, dated, and the record beside it.
(4) ANY VALUE READ OFF THE STANDARDIZED MEASURE. Never per share, never against market capitalisation, never ranked across filers — the price basis is company-specific ('SEC Prices as adjusted for differentials'), the convention swings violently ($0.54 to $1.32 per Mcf at FANG in one year), and the figure can include a large non-controlling interest ($6.6bn of FANG's $36,910M belongs to Viper's 57% minority).
(5) A CROSS-METHOD PEER MEDIAN on any of terms 1, 3 or 4, or on margins, returns, operating cash flow or capex. Segregate or withhold.

AND WHAT WE MUST STOP PUBLISHING TODAY. Owner earnings on this shelf is currently cashFromOps less a capex figure that resolves, for several producers, to a stub — Antero $253.1M against $685.5M of drilling, SM Energy $34.0M against $1,438.0M, Permian Resources $1,070.5M against $1,965.9M, Murphy $29.0M against a $1,020.6M programme, W&T $0.711M against $48.6M. The published owner-earnings figures are 3x to 6x too high. Until the costs-incurred basis lands, gate the line: withhold owner earnings for any producer whose capex is below 40% of DD&A. A withheld number is the correct output; the current number is a wrong one.

## 5. THE LIVE DEFECT HUNT

The Energy shelves ship 130 members across five groupings, all of them on general-purpose column families that were built for manufacturers. The desk's frame — a producer sells inventory it must buy back, and depletion is the cost of goods sold — is not represented anywhere in what is published, and the two lines that stand in for it are both broken in the same direction: too generous to the producer. THE CAPEX CHAIN IS THE CENTRAL DEFECT. It resolves first-tag-wins with us-gaap:PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment and the property-ACQUISITION tags ranked above us-gaap:PaymentsToExploreAndDevelopOilAndGasProperties, which is the drilling. For an E&P the acquisition line is often a rounding error and the drilling line is the whole business, so the chain systematically reads the wrong one: SM Energy $34,000,000 read against $1,438,000,000 of actual drilling; Murphy $29,034,000 against $1,020,611,000; Riley Permian $2,161,000 against $126,000,000; W&T Offshore $711,000 against $48,650,000; Infinity a literal zero against $356,000,000. ### Comparability verdict

YES — and nothing on the page tells the reader.

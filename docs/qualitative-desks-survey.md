## 3. THE MACHINERY

**Fetch/section path (fetchFilings.mjs):** `data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK{cik}.json` → latest 2 of {10-K, 20-F, 40-F} → primary document HTML → `htmlToText` (flat single-space text; re-inserts dropped sentence-boundary spaces) → `section(text, startRe, endRes, validate)` — collects ALL start-anchor matches, guards each against TOC rows (PAGE_AFTER), cross-references (START_XREF_AFTER, XREF_BEFORE, START_QUOTE_BEFORE), bounds at the earliest non-xref end heading, **longest candidate wins unless the validator rejects its content** (`smellsLikeRisk` density test for Item 1). Anchors per form in SECTION_ANCHORS (10-K: Items 1/7/1A; 20-F: Items 4/5/3.D plus UK strategic-report vocabulary; 40-F borrows 20-F's). Ladders below that: bare-title FALLBACK_10K (validated by `headIsProse`), positional `businessFallback` (TOC-end → first real Risk heading), **fragment-primary rescue** (folder `index.json`, largest non-exhibit sibling, per-section merge — the Wells Fargo class), and the **40-F Canadian route** (`fortyFSections`: classify AIF vs MD&A sibling exhibits by whose title stands nearest the top; monolith variant; risk falls back to the MD&A's risk-management block). Quality gate: <1,500 total words → skip loudly. It is section-level raw text — **no inline-XBRL, no DOM structure** (drivers.mjs keeps block structure via `toBlocks`, one level better).

**What a new industry-scoped text lane would reuse:**
- `htmlToText`, `section`, `extractSections`, `fetchText` (retry/throttle/timeout), `folderDocs` — all exported from fetchFilings.mjs.
- `sentences()`/`leadSentences()`/`cleanQuote()` and the guard vocabulary: HYPO (conditional), INTEGRITY_FUTURE (strict declarative-only), BOILERPLATE, ANCHOR, DENIES_CONC — the house pattern is *declarative-past-tense in, hypothetical out, denial inverts the meaning*.
- `bestSentence(sents, want, avoid, prefer)` — the generic one-best-verbatim-quote picker; `integritySentence()` — the strict rare-admission picker; `criticalEstimates()` — the heading-scoped topic-vocabulary pattern (find section, read topics only inside its window: the right template for any industry-specific disclosure section).
- `isFinancialSic` gating (withhold a facet where the industry's language breaks the regexes) — the utilities-desk precedent.
- For number-proved quotes: the whole `src/lib/drivers.mjs` gate stack with caller-supplied XBRL changes — this is the "verification gate that makes fabrication structurally impossible" primitive.
- Ops: `POOL=us|adr|both`, `ONLY_TICKERS=`, `BIZ_DEBUG=` env; carry-over merge (partial runs never wipe); atomic write; `extract.sample/bizHead` diagnostics; `scripts/auditLanguage.mjs` (floors that fail the run; imports the production vocabularies so the audit can't drift); offline tests `extractionTest/candorTest/buffettReadTest/businessTest/durabilityTest.mjs`.

**Known rot/limitations stated in the code itself:** 40-F "no fixed item layout … failing the word-count gate downstream, is simply skipped — the safe outcome"; proxy "table parsing across varied proxies is too fragile" (payRatio only, hence 28%); Scholastic-class body headings only in TOC; largest-filer cross-reference truncation of Item 1 (Walmart/KO/BofA class — the bizHead diagnostic exists because of it); drivers `mdnaText` requires the Item 7 heading in a block **<140 chars** (a heading inside a long block = no MD&A = no drivers, part of why coverage is 19%); `resolvePrimaryDoc` assumes ticker-dated .htm naming; candorPrior is a dead field awaiting the drift test.

## 4. WEAKNESSES PROVEN FROM THE DATA (cite-checked, reproducible)

1. **The litigation regex fires on "defined by SEC" — no word boundaries.** WFC's rendered "Litigation & contingencies" flag is a director-bio sentence: *"Chancy, Craver, Jr., and Sargent each qualifies as an 'audit committee financial expert' as defined by SEC regulations."* I isolated the match: the alternative `(fine|penalty)[\s\S]{0,25}(EC|European Commission|…)` matches **"fined by SEC"** inside "de**fined** by **S EC**". Verified it also survives the display-layer `cleanOwnerFlags` guard — it renders on the WFC page today.

2. **A REMEDIATED material weakness renders as a live cockroach banner.** ADM's `integrity.materialWeakness` is: *"…the Company concluded that the previously disclosed material weakness was fully remediated as of June 30, 2025."* MW_ABSENT's alternation has literal `was remediated`/`been remediated` but not "was **fully** remediated". This renders the Scorecard's gravest banner AND puts ADM in the /flags "restated-or-weakness" register — for a weakness the quoted sentence itself says is cured. AAP is the same class (*"Incremental expenses associated with the remediation of the Company's previously-disclosed material weaknesses…"* — a remediation-cost line).

3. **Drivers ships a product sentence as consolidated revenue.** ABBV's only consolidated row: *"Net revenues for Venclexta increased 8% in 2025 primarily driven by increased demand, partially offset by unfavorable pricing."* — Venclexta is one drug. The anchor `^…Net\s+revenues?` matched, the scope guard only blocks the literal words segment/division/business-unit (not product names), and 8% sat within the 1.0pt tolerance of the consolidated +8.6% by coincidence. Rendered chip: "Revenue · +8.6%". This is the exact masquerade the four gates exist to prevent.

4. **`passedThrough:false` fires on margin-EXPANSION sentences.** OFFSET_NEG's bare `partially` matches any "…partially offset by higher costs" tail. AAPL: *"Services gross margin percentage increased during 2025… primarily due to a different mix of services, partially offset by higher costs"* → recorded as costs-not-passed-through. Same for CSCO and HAYW (*"Gross margin increased due to positive net price impact… partially offset by higher net tariffs and inflation"*). 511 of 704 costInflation stances read false; mostly latent at render (pricingReconciliation prints the squeeze line only when the margin also fell) but the field is wrong in the data any desk would build on.

5. **`pricing.power` false positives.** AAL's power quote is a forward-looking hypothetical: *"While we expect these requirements to increase our fuel costs, it remains uncertain… whether those costs can be passed on to customers…"* (FORWARD is not in the power avoid-list; HYPO's `whether (we|the)` misses "whether those"). AAOI's is the company as **buyer**: *"In some cases, we are able to negotiate more favorable pricing from our raw material suppliers."*

6. **`candor.admissions` false positives in a 52-company field where each one is a large fraction.** BMNR/BMNP: *"We depreciate miners, containers and related site equipment over estimated useful lives of 2 10 years."* — ADMIT's `over[\s-]?estimated` matches "**over estimated** useful lives". APAM's is a definitional sentence (*"Value-added is the amount… has outperformed or underperformed its respective benchmark"*). Both render in OwnersRead as "Does management own its misses?".

7. **`aiRead` reads executive bios and IT boilerplate as AI evidence.** WFC's `capabilityQuote` is an officer listing: *"Saul Van Beurden (age 56) Senior Executive Vice President… and Head of Artificial Intelligence since November 2025…"* — shown in MoatUnderAI as "framed as a capability". ABBV's `inRisk` (competitive) quote is an IT-failure/adoption risk, not AI-as-competitor: *"AbbVie depends on information technology and a failure of… those systems, or a failure to adequately adopt emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence…"*.

8. **Page-number/heading glue reaches rendered quotes.** WFC pricing flag: *"Expectations for other roles… vary by business need. 2 Competition The financial services industry is highly competitive."* (running page header "2" + heading "Competition" fused, two sentences joined). AAON customer-concentration quote ends *"…Air Control Concepts. 9 For the years"* — glued and then truncated at the 300-char cap mid-figure, so the concentration number the lens requires isn't even in the shown quote.

9. **Section extraction thinness is measurable and sector-shaped.** 156 companies carry <200 words of Business, 179 <200 of Risk (WFC's risk = 320 words: the fragment-primary rescue recovered Business and MD&A but not Risk), 56 <200 of MD&A; 599 names (17.1%) have no filed lede and fall back; 58 US names have no language entry at all (AKBA, AL, ALE, AMED, ANSS…).

10. **Staleness/universe drift.** 22 entries in language.json are in NEITHER pool today (CATX, CP, DB, ENB, IMO, LHX, NRP…) — the in-universe cleanup only runs at fetch time and the file predates July's pool changes. Both files are asOf 2026-07-09.

11. **Pool asymmetries a desk must plan around.** ADR entries carry no `comp` ever (no DEF 14A — by design, undisplayed rather than withheld-with-reason) and near-zero `debtMaturity` (20/662). `buffettRead.pricing` is withheld for all SIC 6000–6799 (verified 0) — the financial desks get NO pricing facet and must build their own vocabulary if they want one. Drivers: 19% of US, 0% of ADR/JP.

The pattern across every proven failure: the regexes are one-directional (they test for presence of a phrase, and the negation/context guards enumerate literal variants that filers outrun — "fully remediated", "partially offset by", "over estimated", "defined by SEC"). The structurally sound lanes are the ones where a number must tie out (debtMaturity's reconciliation, drivers' figure check, customerConcentration's display-layer parse) — and even there, ABBV shows a tolerance coincidence can defeat a figure check without a subject check. A new desk lane should anchor on the drivers-style verification gate, not the flag-style vocabulary test.

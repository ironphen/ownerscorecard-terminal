# The AI-Native Direction — strategy memo (2026-07-17)

**Question (founder):** how does OSC go from a free terminal whose surfaces overlap Koyfin/ROIC.ai-class
tools to something entirely new — the best AI-native equity research product — fully aligned with
Graham and Buffett, embracing frontier capabilities intelligently as they release?

**Method:** grounded three-lane workflow (web-researched 2026 consensus map → candidates derived
strictly from OSC's unique assets → adversarial kill per candidate). 14 candidates; 4 kill verdicts
completed by agents, 10 completed by hand against the same map during a classifier outage (marked ✋).
The founder's meta-lesson governed throughout: brainstorming "what to build" with AI returns funded
consensus by construction — novelty must derive from assets nobody else holds.

---

## 1. The consensus map (what NOT to build — fully cited in the workflow output)

Nine lanes, every one funded, shipped, or both, mid-2026:

1. **Answer-engine finance** (Perplexity Finance): real-time, partner-aggregated (FactSet/S&P/Quartr/
   Fiscal.ai), verdict-adjacent; CEO-admitted stale-data problems, documented wrong prices, and a
   41%-hallucination multi-model audit across finance AI. Do not build anything real-time or quote-fed.
2. **Institutional filing-copilots**: consummated — Fintool **acquired by Microsoft** (Apr 2026);
   AlphaSense **$7.5B / $600M ARR**; Hebbia; Quartr AI. Microsoft now owns the endgame (Office itself).
3. **AI junior bankers** (Rogo $75M, Sequoia follow-on; Anthropic's own finance-agent templates):
   forecast-native by definition — doctrinally forbidden territory, and being commoditized by the labs.
4. **"Auditable data layer for AI"** — the closest attack on our correctness asset: **Daloopa $47M
   Series C** explicitly sells filing-linked auditable data to AI vendors (+71pp grounding benchmark).
   OSC must never position as a data API; the gauntlet's novelty survives only as a PUBLIC EDITORIAL
   RECORD, not infrastructure.
5. **The MCP land rush**: a commodity shelf of finance MCP servers with near-zero standalone traction
   (ROIC.ai: 60k tickers, ~2 GitHub stars). MCP is a door, not a product. Enterprise MCP traction is
   partnership-routed (Moody's/LSEG/Daloopa into Claude/Copilot).
6. **Brokerage AI** (Robinhood Cortex "what's coming next", agentic trading via MCP; Public.com
   auto-trading Agents): the anti-OSC — AI-suggested action at retail scale. Strongest
   differentiation-by-contrast we have.
7. **Agentic investing startups** (GIM et al.): signal generators; late-stage capital concentration —
   the entry window in that lane is closing, and none of them keeps a public dated record.
8. **Retail incumbents bolting on AI verdicts** (GuruFocus GuruAI, stockanalysis forecasts) — they
   moved FURTHER from the verdict-free lane. Koyfin notably abstains from agentic AI entirely.
9. **What answer engines actually cite**: head fundamental queries are now PARTNER-ROUTED
   (Perplexity→Fiscal.ai etc.), not organically cited. The machine doors earn the long tail and the
   checkable-record queries — not "AAPL revenue."

**The verified empty cells (no funded player found):** (1) a public, inspectable correctness record;
(2) verdict-REFUSING AI; (3) ex-ante machine-verifiable public ledgers of a publication's judgment;
(4) reader-owned research records as covenant; (5) status-by-record-aging community. These five map
one-to-one onto OSC's assets. That is the strategy.

## 2. The thesis in one paragraph

The funded consensus is *an assistant that answers and increasingly acts* — accountable to nobody, in
public, ever. Every dollar in that lane makes opinions cheaper and less trustworthy, which makes the
scarce goods **verification, refusal, and the dated record** — the three things OSC's doctrine already
mandates and its pipeline already produces. The AI-native OSC is therefore not a chat product. It is
the publication whose **record defends itself in public**, whose **AI is the reader's clerk and the
figures' adversary — never the judge**, and whose readers graduate their private dated appraisals into
the only **community of checkable records** in the industry. Every frontier model release makes the
adversaries stronger, the clerks cheaper, and the records more valuable — capability waves compound FOR
us, against the opinion lane.

## 3. The three braids (candidates, with verdicts)

### Braid A — The record that defends itself (publisher + machine side)
- **Figure Passport → the Challenge Ledger** *(agent-judged: SURVIVES_NARROWED, rank 2)*: per-figure
  named-gate attestations in the existing JSON twins (publication metadata, never an API), plus an open
  challenge door — structured, evidence-required, chronologically logged, seeded from the campaign's
  adjudicated cases. Zero-wrong-numbers stops being a claim and becomes a continuously-attacked,
  publicly-survived property. Only rational to open if you expect to win; only OSC can expect to win.
- **The Model-Wave Re-Audit Ritual** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 2)*: every frontier release is enlisted as a
  fresh adversary over the whole corpus; results published as a dated audit entry. Institutionalizes
  the founder's "embrace capabilities as they release" — each wave is a free, stronger auditor, funded
  by the labs' capex. Competitors experience releases as churn; we experience them as scrutiny survived.
- **The Note That Verifies Itself** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 2)*: each Note ships its machine-readable fact
  base (claim → filing cite → verified figure) plus an "audit this Note" protocol in llms.txt inviting
  the reader's own model to hostilely verify every sentence. The internal loop, externalized as the
  guarantee. Directly feeds Notes conversion — the only publication designed to be read through a model.
- **The Ledger Protocol** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 2)*: ledger.json gains content hashes, publish-commit
  SHAs, timestamps — ex-ante, complete-denominator, checkable by any agent, graded by nobody. The
  red-team's sole surviving thesis, made machine-legible. "The only record an agent can verify."
- **Trap-cases evidence page** *(agent-judged: rank 4, narrowed)*: 20–30 adjudicated extraction traps
  as the standing proof page — never a leaderboard (that slot is taken: Vals/Microsoft, FinanceBench),
  never the full frozen suite.

### Braid B — The reader's clerk (reader side; the retention engine)
- **The Snapshot Reconciler** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 1 — the first build)*: "what the filings changed
  since you last judged" — a deterministic diff between the reader's dated immutable Notebook snapshot
  and the current verified record. No competitor has anything to diff (they hold positions, not dated
  beliefs). Graham verbatim: he compared 396 to 410 himself. **No-AI v1 in days**; makes every
  notebook snapshot compound in value on every subsequent filing, forever.
- **The Owner's Filing Clerk** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 2)*: filing-triggered as-filed delta briefs scoped
  to the reader's device-local Statement holdings — the first monitoring product for owners, not
  traders ("Apple filed; your look-through earnings changed as follows" vs "AAPL moved 3%"). First
  brick = Statement-scoped wire filtering (Wave-1 item, days).
- **The Reader's Own Analyst** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 2)*: published export schema + a `my-records-review`
  skill that runs in the READER'S assistant over THEIR exported records against our verified as-filed
  side. Privacy-fenced by the published commitment — server-side competitors structurally cannot copy
  it. Bright line: presents deltas, never suggests assumptions.
- **Per-ticker wire feeds** *(agent-judged: rank 3, narrowed)*: cheap plumbing so the recipes' canonical
  URLs are ours (the trigger itself is commodity — EDGAR has per-CIK Atom feeds; the moat is the
  verified-record + notebook payload the trigger activates).

### Braid C — The community of records (the moat; the direction A and B feed)
- **Reader ledgers with the notary AI** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 1 as DIRECTION)*: opt-in publication of a
  reader's dated snapshots as a public, ex-ante, complete-denominator personal record — hash-chained,
  deletions impossible, denominator displayed. Status accrues by record AGE and COMPLETENESS, never by
  returns; the platform counts, never scores. AI's only role: notary (attests datedness, completeness,
  immutability). This is empty cell #5, the founder's proven design edge, and the network effect
  nothing in the funded landscape touches — because it doesn't scale like an agent, it AGES like a
  record. Sequenced after Braid B proves engagement.

### Governance gate (before any machine surface)
- **The Machine Readers Policy** ✋ *(SURVIVES — prerequisite)*: the one-page published decision ROIC.ai
  never made: verified records free/CORS-open/citation-optimized forever; the Notes corpus
  agent-accessible to SUBSCRIBERS (their assistant reads what they paid for, verbatim, never
  summarized by us); reader exports never paywalled; agent-side payment (x402-class) a future option
  for non-subscriber agents. Write it first; it is the doctrine gate for everything above.

### Held with care (not first)
- **The Refusal-Native Clerk** ✋ *(SURVIVES_NARROWED — later)*: the on-terminal clerk that answers
  only from verified figures + verbatim passages and refuses verdicts BY NAME is empty cell #2 and the
  category's only-one-of-its-kind claim — but it carries inference cost against the $0 principle and
  prose-drift risk against the register. Build after A and B, tool-forced, usage-bounded, with the
  refusal taxonomy page published first (the taxonomy page itself is cheap and can ship early).
- **The Doctrine as a distributable Skill** ✋ *(SURVIVES, rank 3)*: SKILL.md teaching any agent to read
  records the Graham-Buffett way, refusals included — cheap evangelism of the method, no compounding
  moat of its own. Do opportunistically.

## 4. Sequencing (first bricks, in order)

1. **Machine Readers Policy** page (a day) — the gate.
2. **Snapshot Reconciler no-AI v1** (days) — the Notebook starts compounding immediately.
3. **Ledger Protocol fields** + per-figure gate attestations in JSON twins (days).
4. **Statement-scoped wire filtering** (days — the Wave-1 item, pointed at owners).
5. **Note fact-bases** for the two ledger entries + the audit protocol (a week).
6. **Challenge door + adjudication ledger** seeded from campaign cases (a week).
7. **First Model-Wave Re-Audit** — event-driven, at the next frontier release.
8. **Export schema + my-records-review skill** (a week).
9. **Community of Records opt-in** — when B shows readers returning every filing.

## 5. What we are explicitly NOT building (decided, dated)

No chat assistant as the product. No real-time anything. No data API / "trusted data for AI" B2B (that
is Daloopa's funded lane and the founder's own killed thesis). No model-building or memo-drafting
agents. No alerts framed as action, digests of "what's coming," or theme baskets. No public benchmark
leaderboard. No MCP-as-strategy (doors are plumbing; the record is the product). And no AI, anywhere,
ever, that answers "is X a buy" — that refusal, printed and enforced, is the brand.
